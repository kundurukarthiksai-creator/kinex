Models are bundled locally for dependable viewing. They are existing library assets, not freshly AI-generated geometry.

## Kinex cruise ship

CruiseShip.glb is an original procedural model authored for this Kinex fork. Its geometry and eight materials are reproducible using `node scripts/models/build-ship.mjs`. It uses no external images or model geometry. This design is offered as a deterministic model, not a new AI-generated asset for every request.
